Who is responsible

Roonast Games is the developer name used by Michael Rowntree. Michael Rowntree is responsible for the personal information handled by Roonast Games as described in this policy. You can contact us at roonast@gmail.com.

Playing offline

Every game in Offline Travel Games works without a Google account or internet connection. This includes computer opponents, two-player games on the same device, saved games and local records. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and is never required to play.

The app does not send gameplay data to Roonast Games or connect to our servers. It has no advertising, subscriptions, Roonast analytics or tracking tools. It does not provide cloud saves or online multiplayer. Music is included with the app.

Google Play handles purchases and downloads under Google's policies. The game does not ask for payment-card details.

Google Play Games availability

Google Play Games is disabled in the current release. The integration has been implemented for Android but has not been activated. This version does not initialise the Play Games SDK or authenticate players or submit achievements and scores through it. Local achievements remain available.

The following sections explain how the optional integration works if it is activated in a future release. We will update this policy's availability information before that release. Offline gameplay will remain available without signing in.

Optional Google Play Games

When the service is activated and available, Google may automatically authenticate an existing Play Games profile. This app suppresses automatic prompts to create a new profile; you can choose Connect in Achievements or Leaderboards. Authentication failure or declining to connect does not prevent local play.

The app reads your game-specific Google player identifier and display name. It shows the name while connected and keeps the identifier on your device to assign progress and pending submissions to the correct profile. The display name is held in memory, not saved by this app. The app does not request your Google email address, friends list, avatar image or access to unrelated Google services. Google's own screens may show profile imagery.

Authentication alone does not enable this app's gameplay submissions. Choosing “Enable Sharing for New Games” gives permission to associate new solo games with the displayed Google profile and submit:

  • Achievement identifiers and progress towards achievements.
  • Eligible completed Solitaire times, with separate Draw One and Draw Three leaderboards. Leaderboard times require a qualifying completed game without Undo; interrupted sessions may be ineligible.

Earlier local history, already-started games that were not linked to a profile, and pass-the-device two-player games are not imported. Your local nickname and saved board positions are not submitted. Providing permission is optional; declining it leaves all offline games available.

Pending submissions and stopping sharing

If a connection is interrupted, eligible progress can remain on the device for later submission to the same profile. Switching Google profiles does not move that progress to the new account. The app keeps combined achievement progress and best eligible times, rather than an unlimited list of submissions. It stores up to four profile links locally. It can read your Google achievement progress to preserve achievements already earned there; this does not replace local game records.

Choose “Stop Sharing” for the connected profile to pause this app's future achievement and score submissions for that profile. An upload already sent may still complete. Queued progress is retained locally and may be submitted if you enable sharing again for that profile. Unlinked games are not imported when you do so. Reset Local Data removes retained local queues when the reset succeeds.

If the app cannot save your changed sharing choice, it pauses sharing for the current session and shows a warning while retrying. Until that warning clears, the choice may not survive an app restart. Check the displayed sharing state after restarting, or use your device's app-storage controls to clear local data if storage problems continue.

Stop Sharing does not sign you out of Google or stop Google's own authentication and SDK processing. Account selection, automatic sign-in, profile visibility and deletion of Google data are controlled through Google Play Games and your Google account settings. Withdrawing permission does not undo processing that already took place.

Google's handling of information

Google is separately responsible for information it processes through Play Games Services under its Privacy Policy. When the SDK is active, Google also collects analytics and diagnostics for SDK stability and product improvement. This processing is separate from our optional achievement and score submissions and is not controlled by Stop Sharing.

Google's Play Games SDK disclosure describes gamer identity, achievements and scores, HTTPS encryption in transit, visibility and deletion controls. Other players' ability to see your game data depends on your Google profile settings. Roonast Games does not determine Google's retention periods or visibility settings.

Information saved on your device

The app saves the following locally to provide the features you use:

  • Your optional nickname, which defaults to “Player”. Player 2 has a generic label rather than a separate saved name.
  • Preferences, including theme, card backs, sound and music volume, accessibility options and game setup choices.
  • Saved game positions, elapsed time, moves and actions, limited undo history and local session identifiers.
  • Results, dates, scores and statistics, aggregate records and nickname snapshots associated with past games and best results.
  • The bundled music playlist's playback position and order, so music can resume.
  • Local achievement progress and records of achievement events. If Google Play Games is activated, this also includes linked Google player identifiers, sharing choices and pending achievement or leaderboard submissions.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photographs, microphone or advertising identifier. It checks whether other music is playing to avoid interrupting it; it does not read your external music library or track details. The Android package includes internet and network-state permissions for optional online functionality. These permissions do not make an internet connection necessary for gameplay.

Local saves use the app's storage on your device. They are not separately encrypted by the game. Protect access to your device using its security controls. Google's stated HTTPS protection concerns data sent to its service, not encryption of local game files.

Local retention and deletion

Local saves and preferences have no automatic time-based expiry. Each game keeps up to 100 recent results, replacing older individual results as new ones are recorded. Aggregate totals and best records can remain longer. Changing your nickname does not change nickname snapshots already attached to saved games or records.

Use Options → Reset Local Data to clear saved games, records, preferences, local achievements, Google profile links and pending uploads and restore their defaults. The app replaces both local save generations for those categories. The music playback position and playlist order are preserved so playback can continue. If the app reports an incomplete reset, some information may remain; retry or clear the app's storage through your device settings.

Reset Local Data does not delete information already sent to Google. It is not a guarantee of forensic erasure or removal of copies made outside the app. You can use your device's app-storage controls to remove local app data, including the music playback information. Because Roonast Games does not receive your local saves, we cannot retrieve or remotely delete them for you.

To delete this game's Google Play Games data or your Play Games profile, use your Play Games profile or Google Account controls. Stop sharing and successfully clear retained local data first if you do not want local progress submitted again. These are Google's controls; we cannot delete Google account information on your behalf. The app does not create a separate Roonast account.

Android backups and transfers

The Android release disables app backup through its manifest settings. Operating-system behaviour and manufacturers' transfer tools are outside the app's control. Some manufacturers may allow device-to-device transfer despite these settings. Manage backups and transferred copies through your device's controls. See Android's backup documentation.

Contacting support

If you email roonast@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use them to answer your request and investigate reported issues. Please include only relevant information; support does not require your local saves or sensitive personal information. If you do not provide enough information, we may be unable to resolve your question. We do not use support correspondence for marketing.

We use Gmail to handle correspondence, so Google processes email under its Privacy Policy. Support messages are separate from the app's local data. Google may process information outside your country; its policy explains processing locations and the safeguards it uses for international transfers.

We delete support correspondence when it is no longer needed, normally no later than 12 months after the request is resolved. This allows time for related follow-up questions. We delete it sooner when it is no longer needed or when a valid deletion request applies. If specific information must be retained to meet a legal obligation or deal with an ongoing dispute, we keep only what is needed for that purpose, review the need regularly and delete it when that reason ends. Google's own backup and retention practices are governed by its policies.

Your privacy rights

Where UK or EU data-protection law applies, we rely on performance of our contract with you to provide the local game features you request. When optional Google Play Games is active, our legitimate interests in showing the connected profile and keeping accounts separate underpin our use of the profile information returned by Google. We rely on your consent for this app's optional achievement and leaderboard submissions. We handle support correspondence on the basis of our legitimate interests in answering requests and resolving problems, and on legal obligations where we must retain particular records. Google determines the legal grounds for its separate processing.

You may ask us for access to, correction or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to restrict its use. You may also have a right to receive information in a portable format where the law provides for it. These rights depend on the circumstances and do not require us to collect local game data that we do not already hold.

You have the right to object to our use of your personal information based on legitimate interests, including our handling of support correspondence and Google profile information. Contact roonast@gmail.com to object or exercise your rights. You can withdraw permission for gameplay submissions using Stop Sharing as explained above.

We will respond without undue delay, normally within one month. We may need proportionate information to confirm your identity. If the law permits extra time or requires us to retain information, we will explain why. You can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office or your local data-protection authority. We do not use your information for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
